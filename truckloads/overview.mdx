## Where to start

The Truckloads dashboard is the overview. It can show recent transport totals, latest transport updates and period performance. See [Understand the dashboard](/start/dashboard).

From the dashboard you can open:

- A [transport on the shipment list](/truckloads/shipments)
- The [detail of one transport](/truckloads/shipment-detail)
- The [performance report](/truckloads/performance) when that report is shown

<Info>
  You cannot create a truckload in the portal. Truckloads does not use the Parcels or Pallets claim and ticket forms. If a transport is missing, check the order number, origin, destination and date range first. Then follow [A shipment cannot be found](/support/shipment-not-found).
</Info>
