Use a parcel ticket when you need an investigation and the issue is not a formal claim. Tickets and claims are separate lists.

## Review existing tickets

The ticket list can show ticket number, subject, category, priority, status and last update.

You can filter, export the filtered list, open a conversation or create a ticket.

## Create a ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Parcel tickets">From the Parcels workspace, open Tickets and start a new ticket.</Step>
  <Step title="Look up the order">Enter the complete order number. The portal will not continue if the order is unknown.</Step>
  <Step title="Select one or more barcodes">Choose the track-and-trace numbers that belong to the issue. You must select at least one.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose the issue type">Select a category and then a subcategory. Changing the category clears an incompatible subcategory.</Step>
  <Step title="Describe the issue">Write what you expected, what the portal shows and when it happened.</Step>
  <Step title="Attach files if needed">Each file can be up to 50 MB.</Step>
  <Step title="Submit once">The portal builds the subject from the order number, selected barcodes and category.</Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Screenshot required — replace `/tickets/add`. Mask the order number and barcodes.">

</Frame>

If every barcode on the order already has a ticket, the form is blocked. Open the existing ticket instead.

Some administrator accounts can choose a company context before they submit. Other users submit in their own company.

## Follow the conversation

Open the ticket from the list. The conversation shows the history, comments, attachments and the linked shipment when it is available.

<Frame caption="Screenshot required — replace a parcel ticket conversation. Mask comments and files.">
  <img src="/images/screenshot-placeholder.svg" alt="Placeholder for a parcel ticket conversation" />
</Frame>

Reply in that conversation. Do not open a second ticket for the same barcodes.

See [Follow up on a ticket](/support/track-ticket) and [Information Support needs](/support/required-information).
