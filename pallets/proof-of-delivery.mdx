## Where to look

1. Open the [order page](/pallets/shipment-detail) and check destination, delivery information and expected delivery.
2. Open the [package page](/pallets/package-detail) for the barcode you need. Expected delivery is stored by barcode.
3. Read the [status timeline](/pallets/statuses). A delivered status, or a lifecycle status such as **POD Available**, can appear there when the source has sent it.

If expected delivery is missing, the rest of the shipment can still be correct. Treat a missing value as unavailable.

## If delivery evidence is missing

Keep the order number and barcode and contact Support. Do not create a second shipment.

Use a [pallet claim](/pallets/claims) for damage, shortage or loss. Use a [pallet ticket](/pallets/tickets) when you need the missing delivery update investigated.

<Warning>
  Public tracking can also show destination, carrier, weight and expected delivery. It will not show signed-in labels, claims or tickets.
</Warning>
