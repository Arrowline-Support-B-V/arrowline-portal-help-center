Each entitled module has its own dashboard. Parcels, Pallets and Truckloads data are not mixed in one screen.

After sign-in, the portal opens the first entitled module. From there you can move to the other modules shown in the header.

## Parcel dashboard

Open **Parcels** when you need an overview of parcel activity. The dashboard can show:

- Shipment totals by operational status
- The latest parcel shipments
- The latest returns
- Open parcel claims
- Open parcel tickets
- Service-level summary information

From here you can open the [shipment list](/parcels/shipments), a [return](/parcels/returns), a [claim](/parcels/claims), a [ticket](/parcels/tickets) or the [performance report](/parcels/performance) when that item is shown.

The shipment totals use a recent two-week window. Latest shipments and latest returns are short lists, not the full history. Use [Parcel shipments](/parcels/shipments) when you need to search a longer period.

## Pallet dashboard

Open **Pallets** when you need an overview of pallet activity. The pallet dashboard can show:

- Pallet totals for a recent two-week window
- Latest pallet shipments
- Incoming orders
- Pallet claims
- Pallet tickets
- Performance and country results, when available

From here you can open an order, continue to [incoming orders](/pallets/incoming-orders) or the [shipment list](/pallets/shipments), or follow a [claim](/pallets/claims) or [ticket](/pallets/tickets).

Some administrator accounts can change the company shown on the pallet dashboard. Other users see only their own company. Distributor accounts can also be limited to permitted destination countries.

## Truckloads dashboard

Open **Truckloads** when you need an overview of Full Truck Load activity. The dashboard can show:

- Transport totals for a recent two-week window
- Status counts for Planned, In Transit and Arrived
- Latest transport updates
- Period performance compared with the same interval in the previous year, when that view is shown

From here you can open a [transport](/truckloads/shipments), continue to the [shipment list](/truckloads/shipments) or open the [performance report](/truckloads/performance) when that item is shown.

## Header and account

While you are signed in, the header can show:

- The modules your company is allowed to use
- An unread notification count
- Access to [Account](/start/account), where you can review company information, update your phone number, open invoices or contracts when they are available, and sign out

Use the product that matches the shipment type. A parcel claim or ticket will not appear in Pallets or Truckloads. A pallet order will not appear in Parcels or Truckloads. A truckload will not appear in Parcels or Pallets.

<Tip>
  If you need a specific shipment rather than an overview, go to [Search for a shipment](/portal/searching).
</Tip>
