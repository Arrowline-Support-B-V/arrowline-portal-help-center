Sign in is the secure entry point to the Arrowline Portal. After a successful sign-in, the portal opens only the modules your company is allowed to use.

## Before you start

- The email address registered for your portal user
- Your portal password
- A company account that is allowed to use Parcels, Pallets, Truckloads or a combination of those modules

## Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign-in page">Go to the Arrowline Portal sign-in page.</Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email and password">Use the email address registered for your user. The portal does not tell you whether an email address exists if sign-in fails.</Step>
  <Step title="Select Sign in">The portal checks your details and opens a session for your company.</Step>
  <Step title="Continue to your workspace">The portal sends you to the first module your company is entitled to use. See [Choose your workflow](/start/choose-your-workflow).</Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  You only see the modules and records that belong to your company and role. A direct link does not open a module that is not available to your account.
</Info>

## Forgot your password?

On the sign-in page, use the Forgot password link and follow [Reset your password](/start/password-reset).

## If sign-in fails

See [I cannot sign in](/support/cannot-sign-in) for password, account and access checks.

Never share your password with Support.
