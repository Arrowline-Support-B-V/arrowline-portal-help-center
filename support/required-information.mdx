Include as many of the following as you have. Missing values should be left blank. Do not guess them.

## For every request

- Product: Parcels, Pallets or Truckloads
- Order number
- For parcels: track-and-trace value
- For pallets: barcode or tracking number
- For a truckload: origin, destination and licence plate
- Company and destination
- Date of the shipment or event
- What you expected to happen
- What the portal currently shows
- A screenshot of the page, with emails and personal data masked

## For a claim

- Claim nature
- Carrier
- Article name, quantity, price and description
- Invoice
- Pictures, unless the claim nature is lost
- For a parcel claim with carrier Van Duuren: packing list

## Do not send

- Passwords or reset codes
- Payment credentials
- Unrelated personal information

See [Create a support ticket](/support/create-ticket), [Parcel claims](/parcels/claims) or [Pallet claims](/pallets/claims).
