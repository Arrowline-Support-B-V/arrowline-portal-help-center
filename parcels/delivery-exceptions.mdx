An exception is a grouped shipment status. It is not a claim and it is not a ticket until you create one.

## Check the parcel first

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the shipment list">Find the order in [Parcel shipments](/parcels/shipments).</Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the identifiers">Check the order number and track-and-trace value.</Step>
  <Step title="Read the latest status">Confirm that the row is still in the exceptions group and note the date.</Step>
  <Step title="Check for a return">If the parcel is coming back, open [Parcel returns](/parcels/returns).</Step>
  <Step title="Choose the next action">Use a ticket for investigation, or a claim for damage, shortage or loss.</Step>
</Steps>

## Ticket or claim

- Use a [parcel ticket](/parcels/tickets) when you need Arrowline to investigate a delay, missing update or unclear exception.
- Use a [parcel claim](/parcels/claims) when the incident is damage, shortage or loss and you need a formal case.
- Do not open both for the same incident unless Support asks you to.

<Warning>
  Do not create a second ticket if every track-and-trace number on the order already has a ticket. Reply in the existing conversation instead.
</Warning>
