Open the ticket list in the same workspace where you created the ticket, then select the ticket.

<Frame caption="Screenshot required — replace a ticket conversation. Mask comments and files.">
  <img src="/images/screenshot-placeholder.svg" alt="Placeholder for a support ticket conversation" />
</Frame>

The conversation can show:

- Ticket history
- Comments
- Attachments
- The linked shipment, order or barcode when it is available

Reply in that conversation. Do not open a second ticket for the same barcodes.

See [Parcel tickets](/parcels/tickets) or [Pallet tickets](/pallets/tickets).
