Use a pallet ticket when you need an investigation and the issue is not a formal claim. Pallet tickets and pallet claims are separate lists.

<Frame caption="Parcel ticket list with dummy tickets.">
  <img src="/images/screenshots/tickets.png" alt="Parcel ticket list with dummy tickets" />
</Frame>

## Review existing tickets

The list can show ticket number, subject, category, priority, status and last update.

You can filter, export the filtered list, open a conversation or create a ticket. Export follows the filters and the same company or country limits as the list.

## Create a ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Pallet tickets">From the Pallets workspace, open Tickets and start a new ticket.</Step>
  <Step title="Look up the order">Enter the complete order number. The portal will not continue if the order is unknown.</Step>
  <Step title="Select one or more barcodes">Choose the barcodes that belong to the issue. You must select at least one.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose the issue type">Select a category and then a subcategory. Changing the category clears an incompatible subcategory.</Step>
  <Step title="Describe the issue">Write what you expected, what the portal shows and when it happened.</Step>
  <Step title="Attach files if needed">Add files that help Support investigate, then submit once.</Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Create-ticket form for dummy order AL-100245.">
  <img src="/images/screenshots/tickets-add.png" alt="Create ticket form" />
</Frame>

If every barcode on the order already has a ticket, the form is blocked. Open the existing ticket instead.

Some administrator accounts can choose a company context before they submit. Other users submit in their own company.

## Follow the conversation

Open the ticket from the list. The conversation shows the history, comments, attachments and the linked pallet or order when it is available.

<Frame caption="Ticket conversation without customer names or logos.">
  <img src="/images/screenshots/ticket-thread.png" alt="Support ticket conversation without customer names or logos" />
</Frame>

Reply in that conversation. Do not open a second ticket for the same barcodes.

See [Follow up on a ticket](/support/track-ticket).
