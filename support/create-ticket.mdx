Create the ticket in the same product as the shipment. A parcel ticket will not appear in Pallets. Truckloads does not have a ticket form in the portal. For a truckload, [contact Support](/support/contact) with the transport details.

<Frame caption="Screenshot required — replace `/tickets/add` or `/pallet/tickets/add`. Mask identifiers.">
  <img src="/images/screenshot-placeholder.svg" alt="Placeholder for the create-ticket form" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the relevant module">Choose Parcels or Pallets. Do not mix the two. Do not use these forms for a truckload.</Step>
  <Step title="Open Tickets">Select the ticket list and start a new ticket.</Step>
  <Step title="Look up the order">Enter the complete order number. The portal blocks an unknown order.</Step>
  <Step title="Select the barcodes">Choose at least one parcel track-and-trace number or pallet barcode.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose the issue type">Select a category and then a subcategory.</Step>
  <Step title="Describe and submit">Write a comment, attach files if needed and submit once. Parcel files can be up to 50 MB each.</Step>
</Steps>

If every barcode on the order already has a ticket, open that ticket instead.

For the full product steps see [Parcel tickets](/parcels/tickets) or [Pallet tickets](/pallets/tickets). For damage, shortage or loss use a claim, not a second ticket.
