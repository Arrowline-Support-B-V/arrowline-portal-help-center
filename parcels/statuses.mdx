The portal groups raw carrier events into operational categories on the Parcel dashboard and shipment list. The categories you can expect are:

- In transit
- Exceptions
- Delivered

The label on a row is this grouped status, not every underlying scan name.

## How to read a status

- Check the timestamp of the latest event
- Check the destination and carrier on the same row
- See whether the status is in the exceptions group
- Look again later if a newer event may still arrive

Times come from the event the portal received. They can differ from the time on your clock.

## Statuses are not support cases

A parcel can show **Delivered** while a [claim](/parcels/claims) is still open. A parcel can show **In transit** while a [ticket](/parcels/tickets) is being handled.

| Status group | Typical next step |
| --- | --- |
| In transit | Wait for the next update, or open a ticket if the parcel is overdue |
| Exceptions | Follow [Delivery exceptions](/parcels/delivery-exceptions) |
| Delivered | Close the task, or open a claim if there is damage, shortage or loss |

If the grouped status looks wrong, keep the order number and track-and-trace value and follow [A shipment status is not updated](/support/status-not-updated).
