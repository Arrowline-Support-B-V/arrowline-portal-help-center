Incoming Orders is the pallet planning workbench. It has two lists:

- **Incoming** — orders that do not yet have a latest status
- **Ready to ship** — orders confirmed with status **Ready To Ship**

## Open the workbench

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Pallets">Sign in and open the Pallets workspace.</Step>
  <Step title="Open Incoming Orders">Review the incoming list and the ready-to-ship list.</Step>
  <Step title="Select the rows">Select one or more orders or barcodes that you need to process.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose an action">Download labels, confirm the shipment, revert, or remove only if a correction is required.</Step>
</Steps>

Incoming rows can show creation date, order number, barcode, shipper and destination country. You only see orders for your company and, for distributor accounts, permitted destination countries.

## Confirm Ready to Ship

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select incoming rows">Choose the orders or barcodes that are ready.</Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the shipment">The portal writes **Ready To Ship** for each selected barcode.</Step>
  <Step title="Check the ready-to-ship list">The rows move to the ready-to-ship list.</Step>
</Steps>

## Labels

You can print or download shipping labels for the selected rows. The portal creates a PDF label for each selected pallet.

You can also generate a label from [Pallet package details](/pallets/package-detail).

## Revert or remove

- **Revert** sends a ready-to-ship row back to incoming by removing the Ready To Ship status. Revert can be blocked after the shipment has been communicated to the carrier.
- **Remove** deletes the selected barcode from the workbench. This is a permanent delete, not an archive.

<Warning>
  Use Remove only when the barcode must not stay in the portal. If you only need to undo a confirmation, use Revert.
</Warning>

If an expected order is missing, check the company, destination country and filters. Then follow [A shipment cannot be found](/support/shipment-not-found).
