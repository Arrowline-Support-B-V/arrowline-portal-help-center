The Truckloads shipment list is the main working list for Full Truck Load transports. It shows the transports available to your company.

<Frame caption="Screenshot required — replace `/ftl-shipments`. Mask origin, destination and licence plate.">
  <img src="/images/screenshot-placeholder.svg" alt="Placeholder for the Truckloads shipment list" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Search or filter the list
- Move through pages
- Review the latest status
- Open a transport
- Turn status notifications on or off

## Columns on the list

Each row can include:

- Departure date
- Order number
- Origin
- Destination
- Stops
- Licence plate
- Status
- Last update

The status you see is an operational Truckloads status such as Planned, In Transit or Arrived. See [Truckload statuses](/truckloads/statuses).

## Find a transport

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Truckloads">Sign in and open the Truckloads workspace.</Step>
  <Step title="Open the shipment list">Go to the Truckloads shipment list.</Step>
  <Step title="Set the date range">Widen the dates if the transport is older than the period on screen.</Step>
  <Step title="Search or filter">Use the order number and the origin, destination, status or other filters shown on the page.</Step>
  <Step title="Check the row">Confirm the origin, destination, licence plate and date before you continue.</Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  A truckload is not a parcel and not a pallet. The Parcels list, the Pallets list and public pallet tracking will not find it.
</Warning>

If the transport is still missing, follow [A shipment cannot be found](/support/shipment-not-found).
