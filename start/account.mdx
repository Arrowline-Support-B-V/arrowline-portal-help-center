Account is the signed-in page for your portal profile and company information. It is not a shipment workspace.

## What you can do

- Review the company information linked to your user
- Update your phone number
- Open pricing, invoices or contracts when those documents are available
- Open the notification centre
- Sign out

You only see the company and documents that belong to your account.

## Update your phone number

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Account">While signed in, open Account from the portal.</Step>
  <Step title="Find your profile">Review the contact details shown for your user.</Step>
  <Step title="Update the phone number">Change the phone number if that field is available, then save.</Step>
</Steps>

Password changes are not made on this page. Use [Reset your password](/start/password-reset).

## Documents and notifications

Invoices and contracts open from Account when your company has them. If a folder or file is missing, contact your portal administrator. Do not create a shipment ticket for a missing invoice.

The header can show an unread notification count. Open the notification centre from Account or the header when you need to review those messages.

## Sign out

Use Sign out on the Account page when you finish your work, especially on a shared computer.

<Info>
  Account does not create shipments, claims or tickets. For operational work, return to [Parcels](/parcels/overview) or [Pallets](/pallets/overview).
</Info>
