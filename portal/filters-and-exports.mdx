## Filter a list

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the correct list">Use a Parcels, Pallets or Truckloads list, not a mix of products.</Step>
  <Step title="Set the filters that belong to that page">Only the controls on the page apply to that list.</Step>
  <Step title="Check the result count">Confirm the number of rows still matches the work you need to do.</Step>
  <Step title="Export the current view">Use Export when the page offers it. The file follows the active filters.</Step>
</Steps>

## Filters you can expect

**Parcel shipments** can include date and status filters, together with the other operational filters shown on that list.

**Pallet shipments** can include date, status, shipper and destination filters.

**Truckload shipments** can include date, origin, destination and status filters.

**The Truckloads performance report** uses the period and the origin, destination or other filters that are active on that page. Export follows that same scope.

**Parcel and pallet claims** can include status, date and company filters, plus a date range.

**Tickets** can be filtered and exported from the ticket overview in the same workspace.

The parcel shipment list starts with a limited date range. Widen the dates before you decide that a shipment is missing.

<Info>
  Some pages also let you switch status notifications on or off for a shipment, claim or ticket. That alert setting is separate from the export file.
</Info>

## After you export

Open the file and check that the row count matches the filtered list on screen. If it does not, keep the filters as they were, take a screenshot and follow [Portal errors](/support/portal-errors).
