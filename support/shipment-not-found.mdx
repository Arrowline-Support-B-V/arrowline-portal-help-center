## Check these points

1. Confirm that you are in **Parcels**, **Pallets** or **Truckloads**, not another workspace.
2. Search by the complete order number.
3. For pallets, also search by the complete barcode or tracking number.
4. For a truckload, also check origin, destination and the licence plate.
5. On [parcel shipments](/parcels/shipments) or [Truckload shipments](/truckloads/shipments), widen the date range.
6. For a return, use [Parcel returns](/parcels/returns). Returns are not in the outbound list.
7. For an unconfirmed pallet order, check [Incoming orders](/pallets/incoming-orders).
8. If you only need pallet progress, try [public pallet tracking](/pallets/public-tracking).

If the record is still missing, collect the product type, order number and any barcode, tracking number or licence plate. For Parcels or Pallets, create one ticket. For Truckloads, [contact Support](/support/contact). See [Information Support needs](/support/required-information).
