The Truckloads dashboard and shipment list use operational statuses for Full Truck Load transports. The statuses you can expect are:

- Planned
- In Transit
- Arrived

## How to read a status

- Check the last update on the same row
- Check origin, destination and the stop list
- Open the [transport detail](/truckloads/shipment-detail) when you need the arrival and departure timeline
- Look again later if a newer event may still arrive

Times come from the transport record the portal received. They can differ from the time on your clock.

## Statuses are not support cases

A transport can show **In Transit** or **Arrived** while you still need Arrowline to look at a delay or a missing update.

| Status | Typical next step |
| --- | --- |
| Planned | Wait for departure, or contact Support if the planned time has passed and nothing moved |
| In Transit | Follow the stops on the detail page, or contact Support if the transport is overdue |
| Arrived | Close the task, or contact Support if the destination or timing is wrong |

Truckloads does not use the Parcels or Pallets claim and ticket forms. If the status looks wrong, keep the order number and follow [A shipment status is not updated](/support/status-not-updated).
