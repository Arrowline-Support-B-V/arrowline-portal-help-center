## Where to start

The Pallet dashboard is the overview. It can show recent pallet totals, latest shipments, incoming orders, claims, tickets and performance results. See [Understand the dashboard](/start/dashboard).

Distributor accounts may only see pallet records for permitted destination countries. Some administrator accounts can change the company shown on the dashboard.

<Info>
  Public tracking does not require a login and does not show incoming-order actions, labels, claims or tickets. Use the signed-in workspace for that work.
</Info>
