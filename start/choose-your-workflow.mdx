The Arrowline Portal is organised by the work your company is allowed to do. After sign-in, the home address of the portal is not a mixed dashboard. It sends you to the first module your company can use.

## Signed-in workspaces

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Parcels" icon="box" href="/parcels/overview">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Pallets" icon="boxes-stacked" href="/pallets/overview">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Truckloads" icon="truck" href="/truckloads/overview">
    Use Truckloads for Full Truck Load transports, stops, status alerts and the performance report.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What opens after sign-in

The portal reads the modules assigned to your company and opens the first available workspace in this order:

1. Parcels
2. Truckloads, if your company has it
3. Pallets

The top navigation only offers the modules your company is entitled to use. You still cannot open a hidden module by using a saved link.

## Track a pallet without signing in

Public pallet tracking is a separate page. Anyone with an order number or tracking number can [track a pallet](/pallets/public-tracking) without a portal account.

Parcel shipments and Truckloads transports are not available on the public tracking page. Use the signed-in Parcels or Truckloads workspace for those.
