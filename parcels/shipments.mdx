The parcel shipment list is the main working list for outbound parcels. It shows the shipments available to your company, not returns.

  <img src="/images/screenshot-placeholder.svg" alt="Placeholder for the parcel shipment list" />
</Frame>

## What you can do

- Search or filter the list
- Sort and move through pages
- Review the latest status
- Inspect a shipment
- Turn status notifications on or off
- Export the filtered list

## Columns on the list

Each row can include:

- Order number
- Customer
- Destination
- Track and trace
- Carrier
- Ordered date
- Status

The status you see is a grouped category such as in transit, exceptions or delivered. See [Parcel statuses](/parcels/statuses).

## Find a shipment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Parcels">Sign in and open the Parcels workspace.</Step>
  <Step title="Open the shipment list">Go to the parcel shipment list.</Step>
  <Step title="Set the date range">The list starts with a limited date range. Widen it if the parcel is older.</Step>
  <Step title="Search or filter">Use the order number and the date, status or other filters shown on the page.</Step>
  <Step title="Check the row">Confirm the customer, destination, carrier and date before you continue.</Step>
</Steps>

Export downloads the filtered result, not every parcel in the portal. See [Use filters and exports](/portal/filters-and-exports).

<Warning>
  Returns are not in this list. Use [Parcel returns](/parcels/returns) for reverse-logistics records.
</Warning>

If the parcel is still missing, follow [A shipment cannot be found](/support/shipment-not-found).
