The signed-in parcel shipment list is the main place to review an outbound parcel. The portal does not use a separate public tracking page for parcels.

</Frame>

## What to check

From the list, confirm:

1. Order number
2. Customer and destination
3. Track-and-trace value
4. Carrier
5. Ordered date
6. Latest status

Open the shipment from the list when you need to inspect the selected record. Use only the information the portal shows. Do not assume sender, receiver or proof-of-delivery details if they are not on the screen.
